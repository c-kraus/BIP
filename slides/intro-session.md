# Bias: Not a Bug, but a Mirror

AI learns from data. And data is **the past**.

If historically only white men were hired — the system learns: *That's what a good candidate looks like.*

If women, people with disabilities, or people with migration backgrounds were systematically disadvantaged — **the system learns exactly that**.

This is not a programming error. It is **structural discrimination baked into data**.

![Data patterns](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1568716353609-12ddc5c67f04?q=80&w=2748&auto=format&fit=crop&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&ixid=M3wxMjA3fDB8MHxwaG90by1wYWdlfHx8fGVufDB8fHx8fA%3D%3D)

---



#### Not an isolated case
# Amazon, 2018

Amazon built an AI recruiting tool. Trained on **10 years of internal hiring data**.

Problem: 90% of tech applicants were men.

**What the system learned:**
- CVs containing the word "women's" (sports team, association) → downgrade
- Degrees from all-women's colleges → score lower
- Male-coded language in applications → score higher

**Amazon scrapped the project.** Not for ethical reasons — because they couldn't fix it.

> »The system didn't discriminate. <br> We did — for decades. <br> The AI just scaled it.«

## The real question is not: <br> Can AI be biased? <br> It's: Who gets hurt — and who pays the price?

# GDPR Art. 22 — The Right

**Article 22 GDPR** gives you the right:

- Not to be subject solely to **automated decision-making** that produces legal or similarly significant effects
- To demand **human review**
- To receive a **meaningful explanation**

This applies to credit decisions, insurance, hiring — and much more.

**In force since 2018.** Widely unknown. Rarely enforced.

![Law books](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1589578527966-fdac0f44566c?q=80&w=1374&auto=format&fit=crop&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&ixid=M3wxMjA3fDB8MHxwaG90by1wYWdlfHx8fGVufDB8fHx8fA%3D%3D)

# EU AI Act — The New Rules

Since **August 2024**: the world's first comprehensive legal framework for AI.

**Prohibited outright:**
- Real-time biometric mass surveillance in public spaces
- Emotion recognition in workplaces and schools
- Social scoring by public authorities

**This is not a policy paper.** It is law. <br> Penalties: up to **€35M or 7% of global annual turnover**.



---

<!-- _class: tiny-text -->

#### High-Risk AI
# Recruiting Systems Fall Under This

The AI Act classifies systems by risk level. **High-risk AI** is subject to strict requirements.

**High-risk under Annex III (selection):**
- AI in employment and worker management ✓
- AI in education and vocational training ✓
- AI in credit scoring ✓

**What this means for companies:**
- Conformity assessment before market launch
- Technical documentation & risk management system
- Human oversight must be guaranteed

The system that rejected Sarah? **High-risk.** Without certification, illegal today.

# What Remains

**What ethics provides:** The ability to recognise when AI causes harm — even when it's legal.

**What the law provides:** Concrete rights and obligations — enforceable minimum standards.

**What neither provides:** The decision itself. That's on you.
